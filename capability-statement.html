What I do

EDUINSPECT360 is a regulator-side school inspection management platform for national education inspectorates, UK local authorities and Multi-Academy Trusts. It manages the full inspection lifecycle in one system: scheduling, evidence capture in the field, configurable inspection frameworks, and a multi-stage approval chain from lead inspector through quality assurance to final sign-off and publication.

AI is used to draft — structuring evidence and producing first-draft report text — but every AI-generated output requires named human approval before it can affect a published outcome, with a permanent, append-only audit log of every AI invocation. The platform is built multi-tenant with tenant-isolated, sovereign hosting: each client's data is architecturally segregated, and hosting region and data residency are agreed in writing before deployment.

Founder credential

EDUINSPECT360 is founded and led by Simon Bell, who previously developed the national school inspection system for Jamaica's National Education Inspectorate and earlier worked on Ofsted inspection systems.

Engagement model

Engagements are pilot-first: a scoped, time-boxed pilot deployment with a defined authority or inspectorate, before any wider rollout is agreed. This lets a client validate the platform against its own framework and processes before committing to scale.

Security & compliance

EDUINSPECT360 is being built to Cyber Essentials Plus, SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 standards; certification and compliance status is confirmed in writing before any client deployment.

What this covers

  • Inspection planning and scheduling
  • Field evidence capture, structured against your framework
  • Configurable evaluation areas, judgement scales and inspection cycles
  • Multi-stage approval chain — drafting, QA, school response, final sign-off
  • AI-assisted drafting with mandatory human review and an append-only audit trail
  • Tenant-isolated, sovereign hosting with data residency agreed at contract
