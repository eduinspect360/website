What we do

EDUINSPECT360 is a regulator-side inspection management platform for education establishments. It works at national level by default and is configurable to each client's own structure: national, regional, district or institution level. It manages the full inspection lifecycle in one system: scheduling, evidence capture in the field, configurable inspection frameworks, and a multi-stage approval chain from lead inspector through quality assurance to final sign-off and publication.

AI is used to draft, structuring evidence and producing first-draft report text, but every AI-generated output requires named human approval before it can affect a published outcome. A permanent, append-only audit log records every AI invocation. The platform is built multi-tenant with tenant-isolated, sovereign hosting: each client's data is architecturally segregated, and hosting region and data residency are agreed in writing before deployment.

Team credential

EDUINSPECT360 is built by a group of education and technology professionals who have designed a national school inspection system and worked on Ofsted inspection systems.

Engagement model

Engagements start with a scoped deployment for a defined inspectorate or education body. The client validates the platform against its own framework and processes, then extends it across the organisation.

Security & compliance

EDUINSPECT360 is aligned to ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, Cyber Essentials Plus and the W3C WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standard. Where a client requires formal certification, EDUINSPECT360 commits to achieving it, with the cost passed through to that client.

What this covers

  • Field evidence capture, structured against your framework
  • Configurable evaluation areas, judgement scales and inspection cycles
  • Multi-stage approval chain: drafting, QA, establishment response, final sign-off
  • AI-assisted drafting with mandatory human review and an append-only audit trail
