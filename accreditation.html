The accreditation cycle

From Self Study to annual progress: one connected cycle.

Accreditation bodies often run this cycle across email, shared folders and word-processed reports. EDUINSPECT360 brings every stage into one workflow, with a clear record of who did what.

1

Self Study

Each member school gathers its evidence and completes its Self Study against your Standards and Indicators, inside the platform.

2

Peer Review visit

The Peer Review Team records observations and evidence against your rubrics, on site, as the visit happens.

3

Peer Review Report

A structured draft of each section is prepared from the recorded evidence. The Peer Review Chair reviews, edits and submits the report.

4

Accreditation decision

The accreditation council or board receives the approved report and records its decision, with commendations and recommendations attached.

5

School Improvement Action Plan and annual progress

The school sets out its actions in response to the recommendations, using your own template, ready for its annual progress reporting.

Held, approved, then checked
