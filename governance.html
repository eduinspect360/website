Our commitment to education inspection standards in the UK and internationally. We ensure your organisation's data is handled with rigorous security and ethical oversight.

1. Security Overview 2. Data Residency 3. AI & Ethics

1. Security Overview

EDUINSPECT360 is aligned to ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, Cyber Essentials Plus and the W3C WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standard. Where a client requires formal certification, EDUINSPECT360 commits to achieving it, with the cost passed through to that client. Client data segregation is architectural: tenant identity is resolved server-side on every read and write. Every AI-generated output requires named human approval before it affects any published outcome, and every AI invocation is written to an append-only audit log.

2. Data Residency

Hosting region and data residency are confirmed in writing before any client deployment. Where a client requires formal certification, EDUINSPECT360 commits to achieving it, with the cost passed through to that client.

3. AI & Ethics

Human-in-the-Loop Principle:

Our platform uses AI to identify trends and draft report narratives. However, EDUINSPECT360 does not perform automated grading. All inspection findings must be reviewed and validated by a human lead inspector.
