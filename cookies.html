What this website does and does not store on your device.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Our use of cookies

This website sets no advertising cookies. Our main analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) uses no cookies. We also use Google Analytics 4, which sets cookies only if you select Accept on the cookie banner. If you select Reject, or make no choice, Google Analytics is not loaded and sets nothing. This page explains what is stored on your device when you visit.

2. What this site and its providers use

Item Purpose
Cloudflare Web Analytics Shows us how the site is used (pages visited, approximate country, browser type). It is always on, uses no cookies and does not track individual visitors across sites. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests.
Google Analytics 4 (cookies _ga and _ga_*) Only with your consent. Helps us understand how the site and our campaigns are used (pages visited, how visitors arrive, approximate location). Google acts as our processor. Data may be transferred to the United States under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The cookies last up to 2 years. Our lawful basis is your consent.
Cal.com booking calendar The booking page (book.html) embeds the Cal.com calendar. It may set cookies that are strictly necessary for the booking to work. We do not use them for tracking or advertising.
Google Fonts Our fonts load from Google, so your browser makes a request to Google servers to fetch them.
Cookie choice flag When you select Accept or Reject on the cookie banner, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) so we remember it. It is never sent to us.

3. Managing cookies

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: Cookie settings (also in the footer of every page) clears your choice and shows the banner again. Withdrawing stops Google Analytics loading from then on. To delete cookies already set, clear them in your browser. Most web browsers let you block or delete cookies and local storage through their settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop the booking calendar from working. For how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
