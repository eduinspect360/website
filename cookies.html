Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Our use of cookies

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use cookies to follow you across sites. This page explains what is and is not stored on your device when you visit.

2. What this site and its providers use

Item Purpose
Cloudflare Web Analytics Shows us how the site is used (pages visited, approximate country, browser type). It does not use cookies and does not track individual visitors across sites.
Cal.com booking calendar The booking page (book.html) embeds the Cal.com calendar. It may set cookies that are strictly necessary for the booking to work. We do not use them for tracking or advertising.
Google Fonts Our fonts load from Google, so your browser makes a request to Google servers to fetch them.
Notice dismissal flag When you select OK on the cookie notice, a flag is saved in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) so the notice stays hidden. It is never sent to us.

3. Managing cookies

Most web browsers let you block or delete cookies and local storage through their settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop the booking calendar from working. For how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
