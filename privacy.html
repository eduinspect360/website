EDUINSPECT360 Limited is a company registered in England and Wales (No. 15126870), at 2 Wheeleys Road, Edgbaston, Birmingham, B15 2LD. For the personal data described in sections 2 to 4, we are the data controller. Contact: inquiries@eduinspect360.com.

3. Who handles data for us

We use a small number of trusted providers under written terms:

  • Formspree: contact and newsletter forms.
  • Cal.com: booking briefings.
  • Google: email and fonts.
  • Cloudflare: security and analytics.
  • GitHub: website hosting.

Some of these are based in the United States. Where data leaves the UK, we rely on the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

4. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and briefing records: 2 years after our last contact.
  • Outreach contacts who do not reply: 12 months. Anyone who opts out is kept only on a suppression list, so we never contact them again.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or restrict or object to how we use it. You can also withdraw consent at any time. Email inquiries@eduinspect360.com and we will reply within 1 month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

6. Data inside the EDUINSPECT360 platform

When a ministry, inspectorate or other organisation uses the platform, that organisation is the controller of the data in it. We act as its processor under a data processing agreement. The platform is built to UK GDPR principles:

  • each client's data is kept separate;
  • access is controlled by role;
  • every action is recorded in an audit trail;
  • personal data such as names, contact details and identifiers is removed before any AI processing;
  • hosting is agreed with each client.

7. Cookies

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Our analytics is cookie-free. Booking through the embedded Cal.com calendar may set cookies that are strictly necessary for the booking to work. See our Cookie Policy.

We will update this page if anything changes, and show the date at the top.
