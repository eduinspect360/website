Regional and district bodies

For regional and district education leaders and improvement teams.

Regional and district bodies face a particular challenge: oversight responsibility for establishments they do not control, with limited resources and rising accountability expectations from national government, boards, and elected or appointed leadership.

The challenge

Fragmented inspection data across dozens of establishments. No single view of performance. Manual reporting to oversight boards and leadership. Rising costs of a deployed inspector workforce with no digital coordination tools.

How EDUINSPECT360 helps

Real-time portfolio dashboard. Automated inspection scheduling and reporting. Evidence coordination across your inspection team. Board-ready data exports from your inspection data.

Fewer admin hours. More time in education establishments.

Key capabilities for regional and district bodies

Region-wide portfolio view

All establishments, all inspection statuses, all performance data, in one dashboard, kept up to date.

Inspector workforce management

Schedule, allocate, and track inspector time. Automatically match inspectors to establishments by specialism and availability.

Board- and regulator-ready reporting

Automated summary reports for board meetings, statutory returns, and leadership briefings, configurable by audience.

Internal QA visit framework

Separate template library for advisory visits and establishment leadership support, distinct from your formal statutory inspections but in the same system.

Institution groups

For chief executives and executive leadership teams.

Groups of institutions are accountable for educational outcomes across a growing portfolio of establishments. The expectation from senior leadership and the governing board is clear: visibility, consistency, and continuous improvement at scale.

The group challenge

Individual establishment performance data sits in isolation. Inconsistent inspection approaches across the group. Executive team blind spots between formal inspections. Difficulty evidencing quality at board level.

How EDUINSPECT360 helps

Group-wide dashboard showing all establishments, inspection cycle status, and performance trajectories. Standardised inspection frameworks across the group. Executive reporting packs generated automatically.

Consistent quality assurance, every establishment
International & other bodies

Any country, any level: configurable for any inspection framework.

EDUINSPECT360 is built on a flexible, configurable framework engine. It is not locked to the UK Ofsted model. Any structured inspection framework can be configured, making it suitable for national inspectorates, international education ministries, accreditation bodies, and regional inspection services worldwide.

Education ministries

Configure national inspection frameworks with custom grade descriptors, language settings, and reporting formats aligned to local regulatory requirements.

Accreditation bodies

Structured evidence collection against accreditation criteria. Multi-establishment or multi-campus views. Exportable evidence packs for accreditation panel review.

Regional inspection services

Multi-region or multi-district deployments with central oversight. Role-based access separating regional and national teams with configurable data sharing.
