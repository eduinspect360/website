Strategic need

[Organisation] completes 1,000 inspections a year. Each ends with a written report, and that writing time is inspector time not spent in classrooms. A published expectation in England is to publish reports within 38 working days of the end of an inspection,1 and research published by the FDA in January 2026 reports that 98% of HM Inspectors work beyond their contracted hours.2 [Organisation] needs to return inspector time to observation and get findings to establishments sooner, without weakening professional judgement.

Value for money

On planning assumptions that [Organisation] can replace with its own timesheet data, the platform returns 900 to 1,567 inspector days a year to classrooms. That is 142 to 247 extra inspections with the same team, valued at £307,200 to £534,333 a year at a day cost of £335. Only direct report writing and quality assurance time is counted. In England a contracted team inspector day costs £335 and a quality assurance day £392.3 Costs are set out in the table above.

Delivery

EDUINSPECT360 is configured to [Organisation]'s own inspection framework, grading scale and report structure, so a framework change is a configuration change and not a redevelopment project. Delivery starts with a 45-minute briefing against the framework, followed by a pilot scoped to a defined set of inspections, with benefits measured against [Organisation]'s own baseline.

Assurance

A named inspector reviews and approves every AI drafted section before it affects any outcome, and judgements remain the inspector's. Every AI action is written to an append-only audit log. Data is segregated per client and [Organisation] decides where it is hosted. This matches the human oversight Ofsted publishes for its own use of AI.4 EDUINSPECT360 is aligned to ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, Cyber Essentials Plus and WCAG 2.1 AA.

  3. Ofsted, Payments for contracted Ofsted inspectors, GOV.UK, updated 26 June 2025: £335 a day for a team inspector and £392 a day for quality assurance, covering an average of 3 inspection outputs.
