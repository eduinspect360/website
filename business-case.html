Executive summary

  1. The need. Report writing takes inspector time away from inspection and delays findings reaching the education establishments reviewed.
  2. The change. EDUINSPECT360 drafts each report from the evidence inspectors capture, and inspectors review, edit and approve every section.
  3. The days. At 1,000 inspections a year, the planning range is 900 to 1,567 inspector days a year freed for inspection.
  4. The speed. Reports reach education establishments 7.2 to 10.8 working days sooner, from a planning baseline of 38 working days from visit to published report.1
  5. The control. Judgements stay with inspectors, every AI action is logged, and the next step is a 45-minute briefing and a pilot scoped to your framework.

Strategic need

[Organisation] completes 1,000 inspections a year. Each ends with a written report, and that writing time is inspector time not spent inspecting. [Organisation]'s own target is [X] working days from the end of an inspection. Published targets elsewhere run from 30 to 50 working days (Northern Ireland 30, England 38, Wales 45, Scotland 10 working weeks),1 and research published by the FDA in January 2026 reports that 98% of HM Inspectors work beyond their contracted hours.2 [Organisation] needs to put more inspector time into inspection and get findings to education establishments sooner, without weakening professional judgement.

Value for money

On planning assumptions that [Organisation] can replace with its own timesheet data, the platform frees 900 to 1,567 inspector days a year for inspection. That is 142 to 247 extra inspections with the same team, or the same number of inspections with deeper inspection time, results published sooner, or a lower cost per inspection. As one published reference, England pays a contracted team inspector £335 a day and a quality assurance day £392.3 Use your own day cost. Costs are set out in the table above.

Delivery

EDUINSPECT360 is configured to [Organisation]'s own inspection framework, grading scale and report structure, so a framework change is a configuration change and not a redevelopment project. Delivery starts with a 45-minute briefing against the framework, followed by a pilot scoped to a defined set of inspections, with benefits measured against [Organisation]'s own baseline.

Assurance

A named inspector reviews and approves every AI drafted section before it affects any outcome, and judgements remain the inspector's. Every AI action is written to an append-only audit log. Data is segregated per client and [Organisation] decides where it is hosted. This is consistent with the human oversight principle Ofsted publishes for its own use of AI.4 EDUINSPECT360 is aligned to ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, Cyber Essentials Plus and WCAG 2.1 AA.
